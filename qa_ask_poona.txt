What role did Ambedkar play in drafting the Constitution?

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar served as the Chairman of the Drafting Committee of the Constitution of India. He played a central role in shaping the document, ensuring fundamental rights, social justice and equality. His legal expertise and vision were crucial in building a democratic and inclusive framework for independent India.

What was the Poona Pact?

The Poona Pact was an agreement reached in September 1932 between Dr. Ambedkar and Mahatma Gandhi, ensuring reserved seats for Depressed Classes within the general electorate rather than separate electorates.
